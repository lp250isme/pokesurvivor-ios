App icon files keep the Poké Ball from the PokéSurvivor home-screen icon
published at https://pokesurvivor.com/assets/icons/icon-512.png.

The gold frame and corner marks are removed. They sat on the edge, and the
iOS icon mask cut them off. The ball stays on the icon's original navy field,
scaled with nearest-neighbor to 120px and 180px. GameIcon.png is the ball
alone, on a transparent background, for the in-app splash.
